We Regret To Inform

The Gmail connector of WRTI, a personal job-search tool. Below it is called “the app”.

What it is

A small Windows desktop tool that a job seeker runs on their own computer. It is used by its developer and a few people they know personally. It is not a public product: no website accounts, no server, no payments.

What it does with your Gmail

With your permission, the app reads your Gmail (read-only) to find replies from employers and recruiters to your job applications, such as SEEK notifications and messages from applicant-tracking systems. It then marks that application’s status (“replied”, “rejected”) in the tracker on your computer.

  • It cannot send, delete or change your email.
  • Everything runs and stays on your computer. The developer receives none of your data.
  • An optional AI step, off by default, can send the text of a matched message to your own Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini account to summarise or classify it.

Details: Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Google’s warning screen

Google has not verified the app, so you will see Google’s “unverified app” screen when you connect. Continue only if you know the person who gave you the tool.

Stop at any time

Revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, run gws auth logout, then delete the app’s data folder on your computer.
