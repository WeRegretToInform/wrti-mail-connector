Privacy Policy

App: We Regret To Inform, the Gmail connector of WRTI, a personal job-search tool (the “app”). Effective date: 2026-09-27 Contact: we-regret-to-inform@googlegroups.com · New Zealand · Home page

The app is a Windows desktop tool that runs on your own computer. It is made by an individual developer (the “developer”) for their own use and for a few people they know personally.

Google user data the app accesses

The app asks Google for one permission: read-only access to your Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly, which Google describes as “View your email messages and settings”). It cannot send, delete, move or change email.

Using the open-source Google Workspace CLI (gws) on your computer, the app searches your mailbox for messages from job sites, employers, recruiters and applicant-tracking systems, and reads those messages.

How it is used

Only to find replies to your job applications and mark each application’s status (for example “replied” or “rejected”) in the tracker on your computer. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit or lending decisions, or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your computer:

  • The Google access token is stored by gws in its own folder on your computer, encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); the encryption key is kept in the Windows credential store of your user account.
  • The app’s data folder holds the IDs of messages already reviewed, the sender, subject and date of matched messages, the status derived from them, and temporary working files created during a check.

The developer runs no server and receives no data from you. The developer never reads your mail and has no access to it. The app has no user accounts, cookies or analytics. Besides your own Gmail requests, gws downloads Google’s public API descriptions (the Discovery Service) to build its commands; no user data is involved in that.

Sharing

Your Google user data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, with one exception that you control: the optional AI step below.

Optional AI step (off by default)

If you turn it on, the app sends the text of a matched message to the AI provider you choose, Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini), through that provider’s command-line tool and your own account with that provider, to summarise or classify that message. Only matched messages are sent, never your whole mailbox. The provider handles that text under your own agreement with it; nothing goes to the developer. Before turning this on, check your provider account’s settings on whether your inputs may be used to train its models.

AI and machine learning

Data obtained through Google Workspace APIs, including Gmail, is not used to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your computer until you delete it. To delete it, run gws auth logout (this clears the saved credentials and token cache) and delete the app’s data folder. To stop all access, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored token then no longer works. You can do all of this at any time without contacting anyone.

Security

Your data is protected by your Windows user account and the Windows credential store. Keep your computer and account secure.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is not intended for children and does not knowingly process children’s data.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
